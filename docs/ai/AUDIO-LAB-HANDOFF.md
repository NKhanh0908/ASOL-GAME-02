Product audio code (`src/infrastructure/sfx.ts`, `src/presentation/feedback/audioCues.ts`, `src/audio-synth/`) is **unchanged**; everything below is a dev-only listening prototype plus a decision trail.

| Voice policy | When the voice limit is reached, steal the lowest-priority oldest voice with a short fade; never drop the newest sound. Priority: breath < ui < action < victory. | agreed, **lab bus only** |
| Libraries | Tone.js, soundfont-player and ChucK rejected for now (reasons below). Samples are the fallback if synthesised piano/flute fail by ear. | agreed |

Two existing decisions this direction revises, neither changed in the product yet:

- `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-05-audio-synth-engine-design.md` §3 lists "runtime parameter variation" as a non-goal ("cue logic stays deterministic"). Per-piece voices and idle breathing need it opened. The reviewer must confirm; do not flip a reviewed spec silently.
- `AUDIO_TOKENS.maxVoices = 6` with `sfx.ts` returning early when full (`if (voiceEnds.length >= opts.maxVoices) return;`). That drops the **new** sound, which is the player's action, at the busiest moment.

**Correction to an earlier claim:** `sfx.ts` guards repeats per `cue.key` with `repeatGapMs = 40` (`lastStart`). Chord notes fired **simultaneously** on the same key (`bell`) are dropped; notes staggered by 40 ms or more pass. The prototype's chords are staggered 35-110 ms. So chords do not strictly require changing that guard, only staggering or keying it by (key, pitch). An earlier message called chords "impossible"; that was too strong.

## Measured, and what is still unknown

Measured (throwaway scripts, not committed):

- **IR bug.** Each room was scaled to RMS 0.12, but a convolver's loudness follows total energy. Convolving the `bell` patch at the panel defaults gave wet energy of +25.9 / +17.6 / +13.5 dB over the dry sound (stone / glass / close); wet peak 13.6x / 8.0x / 7.0x the dry peak. After unit-energy scaling: -7.6 / -13.4 / -15.3 dB and 0.3x / 0.2x / 0.3x.
- **Instrument sweep**, ~1500 notes (MIDI 36-98, 44.1 and 48 kHz, neutral and extreme tweaks): no NaN, no clipping, peak normalised to 0.5, no silent note.
- **Pitch**: FFT peak within 2 cents for every note measured.
- **Strings**: three copies detuned in cents were under 1 Hz apart at low pitch, so the fundamental swung ~7x in 1.5 s. Detune is now in Hz with shallow side copies (~2x swing, identical at every pitch).
- Render cost of one note is tens to a few hundred ms in Node; the product would pre-render, as `main.ts` does today.

Unknown — only an ear or a device can answer:

- Whether any instrument sounds good. Expectation, not evidence: glass-type voices (bowl, harmonica, music box) should work because they are near-sine partials; piano, flute and guitar are where additive synthesis is weakest.
- Whether the default wet level is right now that the bug is fixed (rooms sit 8-15 dB under the dry sound at defaults).
- Whether the convolver is affordable in the Android WebView. Not tested on a device.
- Whether idle breathing is pleasant or annoying after a minute. Not built in the product; a toggle exists only in the first prototype.

## Next steps, in order

1. **Human listening pass** on `/audiolab.html`. Record per instrument and per room: usable, tunable, or reject. Start with the comparison button (same note A4 on every instrument).
2. Decide by that evidence. If glass-type voices pass and piano/flute fail: either improve synthesis (add a Karplus-Strong source to `dsp.ts`, pure and portable) or take samples (licence audit first). Do not adopt Tone.js or soundfont-player without a reason the above does not cover.
3. `superpowers:brainstorming` → spec `docs/superpowers/specs/YYYY-MM-DD-<topic>-design.md`. It must settle: reopening the engine's "runtime variation" non-goal; the `sfx.ts` voice policy (steal-by-priority, repeat guard keyed by (key, pitch) or staggered chords); an extended `AudioCue` (pitch, timbre, send); where the shared bus lives (`synthSfxDriver`); the idle layer and its setting; a Android WebView CPU measurement with a cheaper delay-feedback reverb as the fallback.
4. Only after the spec is approved, change product code. `audioCues.ts`, `sfx.ts`, `synthSfxDriver.ts`, `designTokens.ts` (`AUDIO_TOKENS`) and `tests/audioSynthPortable.test.ts` are the files this will touch; run `impact` first and respect the invariants in `ARCHITECTURE.md` (`src/audio-synth/` imports nothing outside itself; `MUSIC_ROOT_HZ` stays out of the `content/audio/index.ts` barrel).
